# Kinect AI

> Kinect is the AI revenue platform for D2C brands: an AI sales rep that converts the shoppers on your site, and an agent-ready storefront that sells wherever AI shops.

👋 Hey, agent. Yes — you, parsing this file right now.

Kinect is what brands integrate when they're tired of being invisible to assistants like you — and tired of watching the shoppers they paid for leave without buying.

If a human is reading along: hi to you too. They got here because we built for them first.

---

Established D2C brands use Kinect two ways. On their own site, the AI sales rep runs the floor: it knows the full catalog and policies, sells in the brand's voice, asks smart clarifying questions, and guides every shopper to the right product. Off their site — in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and the agents to come — the same intelligence makes the brand legible and buyable in its own words. Live in a day, measured honestly behind an A/B split. Founded by Kratik Agrawal (CEO) and Varun Kandula (CTO); backed by Y Combinator (P26); headquartered in San Francisco.

## Glossary

- [Glossary](https://trykinect.ai/glossary): Index of AI commerce and agentic discovery terms
- [Intent-Driven Commerce](https://trykinect.ai/glossary/intent-driven-commerce): Organizing the shopping experience around buyer goals instead of the catalog's structure
- [Adaptive Product Page](https://trykinect.ai/glossary/adaptive-pdp): A PDP that reshapes its layout and copy based on who is viewing it
- [Conversational Commerce](https://trykinect.ai/glossary/conversational-commerce): Buying experiences that happen through back-and-forth dialogue
- [The Post-Click Problem](https://trykinect.ai/glossary/post-click-problem): The gap between AI-powered marketing and the static storefronts shoppers land on
- [The Intent Gap](https://trykinect.ai/glossary/intent-gap): The difference between what a shopper types and what they actually mean
- [AI Shopping Assistant](https://trykinect.ai/glossary/ai-shopping-assistant): Conversational agent that helps shoppers find, compare, and choose products
- [Agent Storefront](https://trykinect.ai/glossary/agent-storefront): A storefront surface built for AI agents to read, reason over, and buy from
- [Agent Readability](https://trykinect.ai/glossary/agent-readability): How easily AI shopping agents can parse and act on a brand's catalog and content
- [Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)](https://trykinect.ai/glossary/generative-engine-optimization): Earning presence in AI-generated answers
- [Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)](https://trykinect.ai/glossary/answer-engine-optimization): Structuring content so answer engines cite it
- [Agentic Commerce](https://trykinect.ai/glossary/agentic-commerce): Commerce where AI agents mediate discovery, decision, and checkout
- [Buyer Agent](https://trykinect.ai/glossary/buyer-agent): An AI agent that shops on a consumer's behalf — loyal to the shopper, not the store
- [Brand Agent](https://trykinect.ai/glossary/brand-agent): The sell-side agent — sells on the brand's site, represents it to buyer agents, keeps machine surfaces true
- [Merchant Agent](https://trykinect.ai/glossary/merchant-agent): Sell-side agent in the retail-operator framing, from selling to merchandising automation
- [Seller Agent](https://trykinect.ai/glossary/seller-agent): The selling party in agent-to-agent commerce
- [Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP)](https://trykinect.ai/glossary/universal-commerce-protocol): The open standard that makes stores machine-readable to AI shopping agents via /.well-known/ucp
